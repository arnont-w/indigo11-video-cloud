# music/: background music

Put music you have the right to use here (royalty-free or your own), named with a mood prefix so `--bgm auto` can choose by topic:

- `calm-...mp3` for gentle or reflective topics
- `tense-...mp3` for danger, mystery or warnings
- `upbeat-...mp3` for fun, rankings or energetic topics

`--bgm <file name>` picks one, `--bgm none` uses no music. The music is mixed very quietly (-32 LUFS) under the voice.
No music is shipped in this pack, because the original sample tracks came from viral songs whose licence is unclear. Without a file here the video simply has no music.
