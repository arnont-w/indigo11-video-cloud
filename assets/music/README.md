# music/ · your own music

Put music here that you have the right to use (bought, free for commercial use, or made with ElevenLabs Music). Use mp3, wav or m4a files.

- Tell Claude: "use the music <file name>". Claude can then mix it under your voice at a low volume.
- No music yet? Ask Claude to find free music, or to make some with AI from a short description. AI music costs money, so Claude shows the price first and waits for your yes.
- Do not use music you have no right to use (popular songs from YouTube or Spotify) in videos you post.
- Claude searches this folder FIRST for background music (by file name, so name files after the mood, for example `calm-piano.mp3`), then free stock, then AI music (with your yes), then buying last.
- `--bgm auto` in the short-video skill picks from this folder first. A name starting `calm-`, `tense-` or `upbeat-` helps it choose by topic (for example `calm-piano.mp3`); other names also work.
