SOUND EFFECTS

Put your own sound effect files (wav or mp3) here. Claude uses them when it edits. Claude can also find free ones for you.
Search order for a sound effect: your own files here first, then free sources (Mixkit, myinstants), then AI generation (it costs credits, so Claude shows the price and waits for your yes), then buying last. Name files after what they sound like (for example pop.wav), because the search matches the names.

Ready-made sounds: the folder "sfx" holds 20 free CC0 sounds (clicks, pops, impacts, swishes, whooshes, a riser).
They are free for any use, commercial included, with no credit needed. "sfx/SOURCES.md" lists where each one comes from.
The three sounds made in code (whoosh, whoosh_small, riser) are the weakest: replace them with your own.

How to add more:
- A single sound: save it here, named after its role, for example "pop.wav". For variants add "-2", for example "pop-2.wav".
- A downloaded pack: move the whole pack folder here. One pack = one folder, keeping the pack's original folder name.

Where to find more (check the licence allows commercial use before you use a sound in paid or sold work):
