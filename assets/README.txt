ASSETS

Extra files the skills can use.

- brand: your brand files (BRAND.md and brand.json). Claude fills them in during install.
- fonts: free fonts (all SIL Open Font License, so commercial use is allowed). Each folder has its own OFL.txt.
    Thai: Kanit, Prompt, Mitr, Sarabun, Sriracha, IBM Plex Sans Thai, Noto Sans Thai
    Latin: Google Sans
  Noto Sans Thai must stay in fonts/NotoSansThai/ because Thai captions use it.
  To use another font, put its folder here and write its name in the Fonts section of brand/BRAND.md.
- images: pictures you use often (products, people, places). Use only pictures you have the right to use.
- videos: video clips you use often (b-roll, places, products). Claude searches images and videos HERE FIRST (by file and folder names, so name them after what is in them), then free sources, then draws or generates.
- music: your own music files. Use only music you have the right to use.
- sound-effects: your own sound effects, plus 20 free CC0 ones in the "sfx" folder.
- templates: looks you reuse (style packs from reverse-engineered videos, or your own). One folder per template, see templates/README.txt.
