CLOUD QUICK START
For a phone, a tablet or any browser. No computer needed.

You need: a paid Claude plan (Pro or Max), free GitHub and Google Drive accounts, and an ElevenLabs key.

ONCE
1. On GitHub, open the pack link. Tap "Use this template", then "Create a new repository". Choose Private.
2. Open claude.ai/code and connect GitHub.
3. In the environment settings:
   - Network access: Limited. Paste the domain list from CLOUD-README.txt.
   - Environment variables: ELEVENLABS_API_KEY=your-key
   Never paste a key into the chat.
4. Choose your repository and this environment, then type: start

EVERY VIDEO
5. Upload your raw clip to Google Drive. Share it as "Anyone with the link". Paste the link to Claude and say what you want. No clip? Just name a topic.
6. Claude gives you a link to the finished video. Tap it, then tap download. Do it soon: GitHub space is limited.
7. Delete the raw clip from Drive afterwards.

Not in the cloud: editing inside CapCut.
Details and help: CLOUD-README.txt
